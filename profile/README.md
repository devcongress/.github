## Builders helping builders

We exist because Africa's builders deserve a community that takes them seriously. That
means no paywalls and no gatekeeping—just people sharing what they know, opening doors
for one another and building useful things together.

## What we're building

| Project | What it is | Explore |
|---|---|---|
| **DevCongress Connect** | Public profiles for Africa's builders: what they know, what they have shipped and whether they are open to work. | [Use Connect](https://connect.devcongress.org) · [View the code](https://github.com/devcongress/connect) |
| **DevCongress website** | The public home for our mission, programs, meetups and community stories. | [Visit the site](https://devcongress.org) · [View the code](https://github.com/devcongress/website) |
| **Events Management** | Open-source event operations for publishing meetups, reviewing talks, managing speakers and learning from attendance and feedback. | [Browse events](https://devcongress.org/#events) · [View the code](https://github.com/devcongress/events-management) |
| **Made in Ghana** | A community-maintained directory of open-source projects from Ghana. | [Explore projects](https://github.com/devcongress/made-in-ghana) |

## Build with us

You do not need to be an expert to contribute. Pick a project, read its setup and
contributing notes, then look for an issue where your experience can help. Code is only
one kind of contribution—documentation, design, testing, research and thoughtful issue
reports all make the work better.

- [Find an open issue across DevCongress](https://github.com/search?q=org%3Adevcongress+is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen&type=issues)
- [Explore every DevCongress repository](https://github.com/orgs/devcongress/repositories)
- [Meet the people already building on Connect](https://connect.devcongress.org/devs)
